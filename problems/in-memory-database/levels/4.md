Add backups.

- `backup(timestamp) -> int` — save the current state of the database and
  return how many records have at least one live field. A field with a TTL is
  saved with the time it had **left** at the backup.
- `restore(timestamp, timestamp_to_restore) -> None` — replace the whole
  database with the latest backup taken at or before `timestamp_to_restore`.
  Restored fields get their remaining time back from `timestamp`: a field that
  had 6 seconds left at the backup, restored at time 8, expires at 14.

A backup always exists when `restore` is called.
