Credits now expire.

- `add(timestamp, amount, expires_at=None)` — a grant can be spent during
  `[timestamp, expires_at)`. At `expires_at`, whatever is left of it disappears.
  `None` means it never expires.
- A charge spends from the grant that **expires soonest** first (never-expiring
  grants last), then from the next, and so on. Grants with the same expiry are
  spent in the order they were added.

Calls still arrive in timestamp order.
