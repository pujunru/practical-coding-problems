Events now arrive **out of order**: the ledger may learn about a credit or a
charge after it has already answered questions about later times.

The truth is the timeline. Apply every recorded event in timestamp order;
events at the same timestamp go expirations first, then grants, then charges
(charges in arrival order). A charge that cannot be covered at its point in the
timeline is skipped.

- `balance(timestamp)` — replay every event recorded so far with time ≤
  `timestamp` and return the balance.
- `charge(timestamp, amount)` — record the charge and return whether it
  succeeds in the timeline as known right now. A charge that arrives later can
  change the fate of charges already recorded; `balance` always reflects the
  latest knowledge.
