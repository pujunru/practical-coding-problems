Formulas reference other formulas, so changing one cell changes every cell that
depends on it, directly or through others. `get` must always reflect the
current contents of the sheet.

A formula that depends on itself, directly or through a chain, is a **cycle**.
Every cell in a cycle, and every cell that depends on one, has the value
`#CYCLE!`. Never loop forever. Once the cycle is broken, the cells compute again.
