Keys come from clients, and clients make mistakes.

**Keys are scoped to a user.** Two users may pick the same key; they are
different requests. Store results under `(user_id, key)`, not under `key`
alone. Otherwise one user's retry could return another user's order.

**A key belongs to one request.** If a user sends a key again with a different
`item` or `quantity`, it is a bug in the client, not a retry. Raise
`ValueError` and change nothing. The original result stays, so a correct retry
afterwards still gets it.

### Review checklist

- The lookup key includes the user.
- The stored entry keeps the request, not just the result, so a retry can be
  compared with the original.
- A mismatched retry raises instead of silently returning the old result or
  placing a new order.
- Validation happens before any state changes.
