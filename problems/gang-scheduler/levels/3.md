Jobs now have priorities, and an urgent job may push lower-priority work off
the cluster — a whole gang at a time.

- `submit(job, size, priority=0)` — higher numbers are more urgent.
- The queue is ordered by priority, highest first, then by submission order.
  Scheduling passes and skips follow this order.

**Preemption.** After the scheduling pass in `submit`, if the submitted job is
still queued and needs more GPUs than are free, look at the running jobs with
a **strictly lower** priority. If
evicting all of them would free enough GPUs for it, evict them one at a time —
lowest priority first, and among equal priorities the one that started most
recently first — and stop as soon as the submitted job fits. Then start it, and
run one more scheduling pass. If evicting all of them would not be enough,
evict nothing.

An evicted job frees all of its GPUs, goes back to `"queued"` in its original
place in the queue, and keeps its skip count. Preemption happens only for a
job being submitted, and it ignores `max_skips`.
