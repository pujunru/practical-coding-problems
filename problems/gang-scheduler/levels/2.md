Small jobs keep slipping past the big one at the front of the queue, and the
big one never runs. Bound how often a job can be overtaken.

- `GangScheduler(gpus, max_skips=None)` — `None` keeps the Level 1 behaviour.
- Whenever a job starts, every job that is still queued **ahead** of it in the
  queue is skipped once.
- `skips(job) -> int` — how many times the job has been skipped.

A queued job whose skips have reached `max_skips` holds the line: in a
scheduling pass, once the pass reaches such a job and it does not fit, the
pass stops and nothing behind it starts. The check applies as the pass runs —
if a start inside the pass pushes a job that was already passed over to the
limit, the rest of the pass stops there.

`max_skips=0` is therefore strict first-in, first-out.
