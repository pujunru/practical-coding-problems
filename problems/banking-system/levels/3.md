Customers can schedule a payment to leave their account later.

- `schedule_payment(timestamp, account_id, amount, delay) -> str | None` —
  schedule a withdrawal of `amount` at `timestamp + delay`. Returns an id
  `"payment1"`, `"payment2"`, … numbered across the whole bank, or `None` if the
  account does not exist.
- `cancel_payment(timestamp, account_id, payment_id) -> bool` — cancel a pending
  payment. Returns `False` if no such payment is pending or it belongs to a
  different account.

Payment rules:

- **Before** handling any call at time `t`, execute every pending payment due at
  or before `t`, in order of due time, then in order of creation.
- A payment the account cannot afford when it is due is skipped (and is no
  longer pending).
- An executed payment counts toward the account's outgoing total.
- Cancelling at the payment's due time is too late: it has already run.
