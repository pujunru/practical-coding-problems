Accounts can be merged, and balances can be read back in time.

- `merge_accounts(timestamp, account_id_1, account_id_2) -> bool` — merge
  account 2 into account 1. Balances add up, outgoing totals add up, and account
  2's pending payments now belong to account 1 (keeping their ids and due
  times). Account 2 is removed. Returns `False` if the ids are equal or either
  account is missing.
- `get_balance(timestamp, account_id, time_at) -> int | None` — the account's
  balance at time `time_at` (after everything that happened at `time_at`), or
  `None` if the account did not exist then. A merged-away account still answers
  for times before the merge. An id can be opened again after it was merged
  away; it starts from 0.
