Add a ranking of who spends the most.

- `top_spenders(timestamp, n) -> list[str]` — up to `n` accounts with the
  highest **outgoing** total, formatted `"account_id(total)"`. Outgoing is the
  sum of successful transfers the account sent. Sort by total, highest first;
  break ties by `account_id` in alphabetical order. Accounts that sent nothing
  appear with `(0)`.
