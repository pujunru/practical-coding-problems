Add two queries over time.

- `keys_at(timestamp) -> list[str]` — every key that has a value at
  `timestamp`, in alphabetical order.
- `history(key, start, end) -> list[str]` — the values written for `key` at
  timestamps in `[start, end]`, in timestamp order, skipping deletes.
